- Viewing decision: capture one completed result per visible foreground chat visit, after synchronization. A newer idle transition during that visit stays unread until a later visit, foreground viewing, or explicit retry. Do not acknowledge results from background synchronization or list selection alone. Native `session.viewed` events request canonical reconciliation.
